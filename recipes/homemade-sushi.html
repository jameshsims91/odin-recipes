Easy Homemade Sushi

Sushi is one of my partner's and frankly my favorite things to eat when we can. Sometimes, we don't want to go out to order because it can take soo long and it can add up quickly. We decided to purchase the necessary items to make it on our own to save money and boy are we glad we did. It's so delicious and I'd like to share my recipe with you, so you can enjoy in the comfort of your home as well. Enjoy!

Ingredients

  • 1⅓ cups water
  • ⅔ cup uncooked short-grain white rice
  • 3 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 3 tablespoons white sugar
  • 1½ teaspoons salt
  • 4 sheets nori seaweed sheets
  • ½ pound imitation crabmeat, flaked
  • 1 avocado-peeled, pitted, and sliced
  • ½ cucmber, peeled, cut into small strips
  • 2 tablespoons pickled ginger

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the over to 300°F(150°C).
  2. Bring water to a boil in a medium pot; stir in rice. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer until rice is tender and water has been absorbed, 20 to 25 minutes.
  3. Mix rice vinegar, sugar, and salt in a small bowl. Gently stir into cooked rice in the pot and set aside.
  4. Lay nori sheets on a baking sheet.
  5. Heat nori in the preheated over until warm, 1 to 2 minutes.
  6. Center 1 nori sheet on a bamboo sushi mat. Use wet hands to spread a thin layer of rice on top. Arrange ¼ of the crabmeat, avacado, cucumber, and pickled ginger over rice in a line down the center.
  7. Lift one end of the mat and roll it tightly over filling to make a complete roll. Repeat with remaining ingredients.
  8. Use a wet, sharp knife to cut each roll into 4 to 6 slices.
  9. Serve with some pickled ginger and wasabi on the side and enjoy!
